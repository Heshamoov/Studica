Three hours on the clock. Two team members. One 2 × 2 m arena.

The objects

Flat pieces — 75 × 75 × 10 mm, lying flat. Colours: red, green, yellow, blue + one surprise hazard colour announced at the morning briefing.
Storage bases — 100 mm cube, 80 × 80 mm cavity. Front face is a stepped slot: an 80 mm top mouth tapers into a 50 × 30 mm side slot whose bottom edge sits 30 mm off the floor — confirmed from the official SC2026_TP23 appendix, not a photo.
Delivery stands — 25 mm / 50 mm / 75 mm / 100 mm high, each a walled pocket (95 × 95 mm inner, 5 mm wall). Pocket depth is 20 mm on the 25 mm stand, 40 mm on the other three.

How it runs: C++, compiled on the robot. This robot has no ROS 2, and the WPILib libraries that ship on the board are roboRIO (soft-float) builds the Pi cannot load. So robot-onboard/ bundles the Raspberry Pi builds and you compile it with the robot's own g++. Every number you tune lives on the robot and is edited from tools/settings.html — nothing to edit in code. Earlier attempts (Python/ROS 2, roboRIO C++) are in old/ for reference only.

How the data flows

Copy + compile on robot→Settings page→Control panel→ Build routine→Paste into Robot.cpp→Start button runs it

The pages send every button to the robot on port 5800 over plain HTTP. One dispatch table (Sequence.cpp) serves the buttons, saved routines and the physical start button, so they cannot disagree. Your calibration is saved on the robot in settings.cfg, so copying new code never overwrites it — five teams can share the same program.

What is built

Gamepad teleop (D1 · 30 marks) — no extension axis to jog any more: the chassis itself drives forward/backward to insert and withdraw the fork. RB/LB drive the clamp servo directly (open / hold).
Camera colour detection (C1 + E1) — Camera.cpp classifies pieces as red / green / yellow / blue / hazard on the same HSV thresholds as the original C++ version. The sort_piece command routes each piece to the correct delivery stand automatically.
Clamp servo (pick sequence) — clamp_open() fires before entering the storage base, clamp_hold() secures the piece after lifting off the slot. Two panel buttons: clamp_open / clamp_hold.

Key dimensions (all in mm)

Storage slot 30–60 (elevator min height 30, matching) · Fork enters at 32 · Lifts to 40 (on piece) · Clears the slot at 65 · Carry height 40 · Stand pocket depth 20 (stand 1) / 40 (stands 2–4).
