Field Map ESC2026 · Skill 23

Court

Drag a chip onto the court to place it. Drag any object to move it. Select one to get handles — walls resize from their end dots, areas from their corners. Delete removes the selection, Esc deselects.

Origin is the bottom-left corner; +x runs along the length, +y across the width, heading 0° = +x counter-clockwise — ROS REP-103, so it matches studica_control without conversion. Everything is in metres.
